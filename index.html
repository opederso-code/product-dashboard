How the calculations work

CPM = ad spend ÷ impressions × 1,000. CTR = link clicks ÷ impressions. L-CR = leads ÷ link clicks. CPL = ad spend ÷ leads. R-CR = reservations ÷ leads. CPR = ad spend ÷ reservations.

Estimated revenue = price × (reservations × reservation purchase rate + eligible leads × lead purchase rate). By default, eligible leads = leads − reservations, avoiding double counting. Choose separate pools only if leads exclude reservers.

eROAS = estimated revenue ÷ ad spend. A 200% eROAS means $2 of estimated revenue per $1 in ads. COGS is shown separately and is not subtracted from revenue ROAS. Contribution = realistic revenue × (1 − COGS rate) − ad spend; this excludes other costs.
